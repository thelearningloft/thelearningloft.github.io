About Us

More Than Just Daycare,
We Are Family.

Welcome to The Learning Loft LLC. Founded by Sarah Jenkins, a certified early childhood educator and mother of two, our goal has always been simple: to create the daycare we wished we had for our own children.

Located in a quiet neighborhood, our facility combines the resources of a commercial center with the warmth of a home. We believe that low ratios (1:4) allow us to truly know each child's personality, learning style, and needs.

Our Philosophy

We believe that children learn best when they feel safe, loved, and free to explore. Our curriculum is emergent, meaning we follow the children's interests to create lesson plans that engage them naturally.
